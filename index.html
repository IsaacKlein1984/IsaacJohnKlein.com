About

Every record has a moment where it comes alive. My job is to hear it before anyone else does — and make sure it reaches the listener intact.

I'm a producer and recording/mixing engineer based in Los Angeles, working across live jazz, orchestral sessions, and modern production. As engineer and right hand to GRAMMY® and EMMY® winner Steve Tyrell, I've spent years in rooms where the standard is nothing short of timeless.

From tracking a big band to managing a tour from load-in to encore, I bring the same care to every stage of the process: attentive ears, calm logistics, and a finished product that sounds as good as the moment it was made.

Services

Production

Artist & Tour Management
